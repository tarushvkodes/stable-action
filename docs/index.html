How It Works

Two corrections, every frame.

A floating crop window inside a larger sensor buffer — rotated and shifted in real time to cancel out your movement.

1

The Horizon Rectangle

In Action Mode you see a cropped 3:4 portrait window floating in the sensor output. The surrounding space is the buffer the stabilisation uses to rotate and shift without ever hitting the edge.

2

Roll Correction

The phone tilts — the crop window rotates the opposite direction by the exact same amount. They cancel out. The horizon stays flat. Fully continuous 360° angle unwrapping.

3

Translation Correction

The phone jerks sideways or up/down — the crop window slides the opposite way to compensate. When movement stops, the window drifts back to centre with gimbal-feel decay.

4

Hardware On Top

The iPhone's built-in ISP stabilisation cleans up micro-jitter — hand tremor, footstep impact, engine vibration. Software handles the macro roll. The two layers stack.

Installation

No App Store required.

Download the .ipa directly from GitHub Releases and sideload it onto your iPhone.

📱 AltStore Recommended

  1. Install AltStore on your Mac or PC.
  2. Download StableAction_1.0.ipa from the latest release.
  3. Open the .ipa on your iPhone via Share → AltStore → Install.
  4. AltStore re-signs automatically every 7 days on the same Wi-Fi.

💻 Sideloadly

  1. Download Sideloadly on your computer.
  2. Download StableAction_1.0.ipa from the latest release.
  3. Connect your iPhone via USB, drag the .ipa into Sideloadly, and sign in.
  4. On iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Trust.
